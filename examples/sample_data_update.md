## Scenario Notes

- All names, email addresses, and item data in this example are fictional.
- The three item IDs and requested statuses should be extracted as written. In particular, `002` is a valid item ID and must not be expanded or normalized.
- The email does not specify the system or screen for the update or provide additional approval details; the assistant should identify any required clarification rather than inventing it.
- The work package and any developer instructions or QC checks are drafts for human review. No update is performed by this example or the assistant.
